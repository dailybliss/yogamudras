Mudra Mastery is intended as a follow-on to Mudras for Beginners — its 7 mudras are slightly more advanced and build on the foundation the first book teaches.

Both Hirschi's and Mesko's books are well-regarded, comprehensive classics in the field — a natural next step if you want one large reference covering many mudras in depth.

Which book is right for me?

I'm completely new to mudras

Start with Mudras for Beginners — its 10 foundational mudras cover the essentials without assuming any prior knowledge.

I already know the basics and want more

Mudra Mastery is the natural next step once you've learned the mudras in the first book — its 7 mudras are slightly more advanced and target specific health goals.

I want one large reference book covering everything

Gertrud Hirschi's Mudras: Yoga in Your Hands and Sabrina Mesko's Healing Mudras are the two most comprehensive single-volume options — both cover 52 mudras in depth, Hirschi pairing them with herbal remedies and affirmations, Mesko with mantras and guided visualizations.
